Automation & Workflow Management

Accessing Automation

  1. Click AUTOMATION → Automation Dashboard.
  2. The four counts at the top (active rules, jobs on, unread notifications, Auto-POs drafted) each open their tab.
  3. Overview shows each active rule's last run and each background job's last result. Anything that failed is listed first under Needs a look.

Automation Rules

When something is true, tell people (or draft purchase orders)

  1. Open the Automation Rules tab and press New rule, or one of the ready-made rules next to it (out of stock, low stock, overdue invoices, late purchase orders, Auto-PO drafts).
  2. Choose When:
    • An item is low on stock (at or below its low stock level; blank means 50)
    • An item is out of stock
    • An item has no supplier part number
    • A watched Auto-PO item is at its reorder point
    • A purchase order is late (still open past its expected date)
    • An invoice is overdue (sent, unpaid and past its due date)
    For items, you can limit it to stock codes starting with something, such as CAP-. Discontinued, inactive and do-not-use items are left out.
  3. Choose Then: tell everyone, the admins, the managers or one person (by email address), in the app's Notifications tab; or, for Auto-PO items, draft purchase orders (what Auto-PO's Draft does: one per supplier, nothing sent).
  4. Choose How often: every hour, day or week, or only when you press Run now.
  5. What it finds shows what the rule matches right now and how many would be new, without changing anything.

People are told only about what's new since the rule last ran. An item that is still out of stock tomorrow isn't announced again; one that comes back into stock and runs out again is. Nothing is emailed: no email sender is set up.

Only admins and managers can add, change, run or delete rules; everyone can see them.

Auto-PO Configuration

Reorder the items you choose

  1. Open Auto-PO Config in the sidebar (or the Auto-PO tab of the Automation dashboard).
  2. Press Watch items, search for and pick the items, and set:
    • Reorder point: reorder when stock is at or below this.
    • Minimum level: order enough to bring stock back up to this.
    • Supplier: one you choose, or Automatic (the item's Supplier field, else the fastest of the suppliers it has part numbers for).
    • Optionally, mark its purchase orders as Sent instead of Draft. You still place the order with the supplier.
  3. Press Check now. It shows what it would draft, one purchase order per supplier, and anything it skips (an item with a purchase order already open, or paused). Press Draft to create them.
  4. Nothing is ordered on its own. Prices on the drafts are each item's last cost; check them before sending.

Scheduled Jobs

The work the app does on its own

  • Exchange rates: daily at 08:00, the day's USD and other rates to rand.
  • Recurring invoices: daily at 05:00, each one due becomes a draft invoice.
  • Bulk pricing: daily at 04:30, re-prices items whose bulk price is too old. Run it by hand from Pricing.
  • Automation rules: every hour at a quarter past, runs each active rule that is due.

Each shows when it last ran, whether it worked and what it did. Turn off skips a job when its time comes; Run now runs it straight away (admins and managers). Times are South African time.

Notifications

The inbox for what rules tell people: yours, your role's and everyone's. Unread or All, Mark read one at a time or Mark all read. The unread count shows on the tab.

Event Log

Everything automation does, newest first, in words: each rule and job run (on schedule or by whom), Auto-PO drafts, and failures with the reason. Filter by kind or result, search, Show older, and Export CSV what's shown.

✅ Start small: add the ready-made Out of stock rule, press What it finds, then Run now. The first run tells people about everything it finds; after that only what's new.

Quality & Compliance Management

Accessing Quality Module

Step 1: Navigate to Quality Management

  1. Click QUALITY & ANALYTICS → Quality & Compliance
  2. View quality metrics dashboard with:
    • Passed inspections count
    • Failed inspections count
    • Open NCRs (Non-Conformance Reports)
    • Defect rate percentage

Quality Assurance Inspections

Schedule and Conduct QA Inspections

  1. Click QA Inspections tab
  2. Click Schedule Inspection
  3. Define inspection:
    • Component/product to inspect
    • Inspection type (incoming, in-process, final)
    • Sample size
    • Criteria and standards
  4. Record results:
    • Accept/reject decision
    • Defects found (if any)
    • Inspector notes
  5. System tracks pass/fail rates

Defect Tracking

Log and Manage Defects

  1. Click Defects tab
  2. Click Report Defect
  3. Enter defect details:
    • Component/product affected
    • Defect description
    • Severity (CRITICAL, MAJOR, MINOR)
    • Root cause (if known)
    • Assigned to (person responsible for fix)
  4. Track defect through resolution
  5. Close defect with closure notes

Non-Conformance Reports (NCR)

Create and Manage NCRs

  1. Click NCRs tab
  2. Click Create NCR
  3. Document non-conformance:
    • NCR number (auto-generated)
    • Issue description
    • Item/process affected
    • Date discovered
    • Immediate action taken
  4. Assign corrective actions
  5. Set follow-up review date
  6. Close NCR after verification
💡 Tip: Use the Quality module to maintain compliance records for ISO 9001, AS9100, or other standards your organization follows.
