Automation & Workflow Management

Accessing Automation

  1. Click AUTOMATION → Automation Dashboard.
  2. The four counts at the top (active rules, jobs on, unread notifications, Auto-POs drafted) each open their tab.
  3. Overview shows each active rule's last run and each background job's last result. Anything that failed is listed first under Needs a look.

Automation Rules

When something is true, tell people (or draft purchase orders)

  1. Open the Automation Rules tab and press New rule, or one of the ready-made rules next to it (out of stock, low stock, overdue invoices, late purchase orders, Auto-PO drafts).
  2. Choose When:
    • An item is low on stock (at or below its low stock level; blank means 50)
    • An item is out of stock
    • An item has no supplier part number
    • A watched Auto-PO item is at its reorder point
    • A purchase order is late (still open past its expected date)
    • An invoice is overdue (sent, unpaid and past its due date)
    For items, you can limit it to stock codes starting with something, such as CAP-. Discontinued, inactive and do-not-use items are left out.
  3. Choose Then: tell everyone, the admins, the managers or one person (by email address), in the app's Notifications tab; or, for Auto-PO items, draft purchase orders (what Auto-PO's Draft does: one per supplier, nothing sent).
  4. Choose How often: every hour, day or week, or only when you press Run now.
  5. What it finds shows what the rule matches right now and how many would be new, without changing anything.

People are told only about what's new since the rule last ran. An item that is still out of stock tomorrow isn't announced again; one that comes back into stock and runs out again is. Nothing is emailed: no email sender is set up.

Only admins and managers can add, change, run or delete rules; everyone can see them.

Auto-PO Configuration

Reorder the items you choose

  1. Open Auto-PO Config in the sidebar (or the Auto-PO tab of the Automation dashboard).
  2. Press Watch items, search for and pick the items, and set:
    • Reorder point: reorder when stock is at or below this.
    • Minimum level: order enough to bring stock back up to this.
    • Supplier: one you choose, or Automatic (the item's Supplier field, else the fastest of the suppliers it has part numbers for).
    • Optionally, mark its purchase orders as Sent instead of Draft. You still place the order with the supplier.
  3. Press Check now. It shows what it would draft, one purchase order per supplier, and anything it skips (an item with a purchase order already open, or paused). Press Draft to create them.
  4. Nothing is ordered on its own. Prices on the drafts are each item's last cost; check them before sending.

Scheduled Jobs

The work the app does on its own

  • Exchange rates: daily at 08:00, the day's USD and other rates to rand.
  • Recurring invoices: daily at 05:00, each one due becomes a draft invoice.
  • Bulk pricing: daily at 04:30, re-prices items whose bulk price is too old. Run it by hand from Pricing.
  • Automation rules: every hour at a quarter past, runs each active rule that is due.

Each shows when it last ran, whether it worked and what it did. Turn off skips a job when its time comes; Run now runs it straight away (admins and managers). Times are South African time.

Notifications

The inbox for what rules tell people: yours, your role's and everyone's. Unread or All, Mark read one at a time or Mark all read. The unread count shows on the tab.

Event Log

Everything automation does, newest first, in words: each rule and job run (on schedule or by whom), Auto-PO drafts, and failures with the reason. Filter by kind or result, search, Show older, and Export CSV what's shown.

✅ Start small: add the ready-made Out of stock rule, press What it finds, then Run now. The first run tells people about everything it finds; after that only what's new.

Quality & Compliance Management

Accessing Quality

  1. Click QUALITY → Quality & Compliance.
  2. The overview is worked out from what has been recorded: the share of inspections passed, the defect rate (defects found per unit sampled), open defects by severity, open and overdue NCRs and how long NCRs take to resolve, and how many compliance checkpoints were compliant at their last check.
  3. Needs attention lists inspections past their date, open critical defects, overdue NCRs, and checkpoints that failed or were never checked. Each opens its tab.

Inspections

  1. On Inspections, press New inspection: the item, the kind (incoming for goods received, in-process during a build, final before dispatch), the sample size, the date, optionally the batch, lot or PO, the inspector (blank: you) and what to check.
  2. Start marks it in progress. Record result: passed, failed or on hold, with the defects found and notes. A failed inspection needs the defects found or a note saying why.
  3. When one fails, it offers to Log the defect or Raise an NCR, filled in from the inspection.
  4. An inspection that hasn't started can be deleted. To do, Done or All above the list.

Defects

  1. Report defect: the item, how serious (critical: unsafe or unusable; major: fails a requirement; minor: cosmetic), an optional code such as BENT-PIN, what's wrong and who it's assigned to.
  2. Move it on: Investigate, Resolve, Close, or Reopen. Resolving or closing asks how it was fixed; the root cause can be recorded too.

Non-Conformance Reports (NCR)

  1. Raise NCR: the item, the supplier (if it is theirs), how serious, what doesn't conform, when it was found, the immediate action taken (e.g. stock quarantined), who it's assigned to and a review-by date. It is numbered NCR-2026-0001, and so on.
  2. Move it on: Assign (asks who), Start work, Resolve (asks for the corrective action and how it was verified), then Close (verified).
  3. One past its review-by date is marked overdue. An automation rule can tell people about overdue NCRs and inspections (Automation > Automation Rules).
  4. A supplier's NCRs count against its score under Advanced Automation.

Compliance

  1. Add a checkpoint for each check your standard asks for (ISO 9001, IPC-A-610, RoHS, AS9100, …): what is checked, the standard, the stage (receiving, production, final inspection, dispatch, supplier, company-wide) and the documents it needs.
  2. Each time it is checked, Record a check: compliant, not compliant or waived, what it was for (a batch, PO or job) and notes. Not compliant or waived needs a note.
  3. The last check is the checkpoint's status; History shows every check. Retire a checkpoint no longer needed.

Advanced automation

Advanced Automation

Open QUALITY → Advanced Automation. Everything here is worked out from your stock, the stock ledger, purchase orders and NCRs; where there isn't enough history it says so rather than show a made-up number.

  • Scan for Anomalies: negative stock, stock-outs with demand, stock below its reorder level, dead stock and spikes in use. On the Anomalies tab, mark each Acknowledge, Investigating, Resolved or Not a problem; a rescan doesn't raise again what has been acknowledged, is being investigated or isn't a problem.
  • Generate Forecasts: each part's expected use over the next 30 days, from its history (it needs 3 or more uses over 7 or more days). The Forecasts tab lists them.
  • Score Suppliers: on time (60%, where both the expected and received dates are known) and orders without an NCR (40%). A supplier needs 3 received orders to be scored.
  • Reorder: what to order, from stock, use and lead times, with the supplier order files.
  • ML Models: Train does what the model is for. A demand forecast is backtested for a measured accuracy; anomaly detection runs a scan; supplier ranking scores the suppliers; defect prediction waits for a history of inspection results. Accuracy is never typed in.

The scan, forecasts and supplier scores also run on their own every day at 08:30 (Automation > Scheduled Jobs: Stock analysis).
